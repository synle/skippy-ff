Skippy

Auto-skip intros, recaps, and credits while you binge.

Master Settings

Default skip rules. Used by every site that doesn't have its own settings below.

Sites

Each site can follow the master settings or use its own.

Advanced

How often Skippy scans the page for a skip button. 500 ms by default; allowed range 100–5000 ms.

Debugging

Off by default. Enable to see scan + click diagnostics in the page's DevTools console.
